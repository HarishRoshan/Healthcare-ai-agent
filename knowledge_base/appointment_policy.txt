
AURA DEMO CLINIC — APPOINTMENT POLICY
Document ID: AURA-POL-001
Version: 1.0
Status: Fictional training document

1. Appointment Cancellation
Patients should cancel appointments at least 24 hours
before their scheduled appointment.

2. Missed Appointments
If a patient misses an appointment, they should contact
the clinic reception to arrange another appointment.

3. Appointment Rescheduling
Patients may request rescheduling by contacting the
clinic reception team.

4. Emergency Situations
This appointment policy is not intended for emergencies.
Patients experiencing a medical emergency should contact
the appropriate emergency service.

5. Information Scope
This document is for the fictional AURA Demo Clinic.
It must not be presented as official NHS policy.
